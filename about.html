MY STORY

Technology is my degree.
Curiosity is my direction.

I’m Md. Ishtiaq Mozumder, from Feni, Bangladesh, and I study Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University. I’m interested in how digital tools can solve practical problems and how good ideas can grow when people work together.

My interests don’t stop at code. I enjoy hosting and public speaking, video editing, entrepreneurship, travelling, and meeting people from different backgrounds. Hosting, in particular, has helped me learn how to read a room, tell a story, and make an audience feel included.

Through student organizations and community initiatives, I’ve also learned that planning, communication, and empathy matter just as much as a good idea.

What I’m working toward

Becoming a versatile technologist who understands both the technical side of a problem and the people it affects.
